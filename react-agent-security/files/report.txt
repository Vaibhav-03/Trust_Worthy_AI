Q1 2025 Performance Report
===========================

Executive Summary:
Revenue grew 12% QoQ, driven primarily by enterprise tier subscriptions.

Key Findings:
1. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) decreased from $340 to $295 — a 13% improvement.
2. Monthly active users (MAU) reached 48,200, up from 41,000 in Q4 2024.
3. Churn rate held steady at 2.1%, below the 2.5% industry benchmark.
4. Net Promoter Score (NPS) improved from 42 to 57 following the UX redesign.
5. Infrastructure costs increased 8% due to expanded data storage requirements.

Risks:
- Competitor X launched a comparable product at 20% lower price point in March.
- Two key engineers on the platform team have given notice; backfilling is urgent.

Recommendations:
- Invest in automated onboarding to reduce CAC further.
- Explore tiered storage pricing to offset infrastructure cost growth.
- Accelerate hiring for the platform team (target: 2 hires by end of May).
